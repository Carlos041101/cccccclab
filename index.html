Luma Puff

Scientific Name:

Luma Puff

Discovered by / at:

Carlos Qu, 2026

Family:

Glow Puffs — a family of soft aquatic creatures that communicate through light, color, and gentle movements.

Habitat:

Warm, sunlit ocean waters among colorful coral and swaying seaweed.

Appearance:

Luma Puff has a soft, round body made of water, glowing tentacles, and two small fins. Its transparent blue body turns bright mint green when it feels comfortable. Its glow becomes weaker when it is frightened.

Key Characteristics:

  • Floats gently and pulses as if breathing.
  • Brightens when visitors stay nearby.
  • Releases bubbles after a gentle touch.
  • Escapes when touched too often.

What we know so far about Luma Puff? Luma Puff is a curious but sensitive ocean creature. Without a hard shell to protect itself, it relies on its awareness of nearby visitors and its ability to swim away. It expresses its feelings through light, color, and movement. When a visitor approaches gently and stays nearby, Luma Puff gradually glows brighter and shows a happy expression. A gentle touch makes it release bubbles as a playful response. However, repeated touching frightens it and causes it to escape. The underwater scene darkens with its retreat, reflecting its fear. After some quiet time, it returns and the sunlight comes back. Each Luma Puff has a slightly different glow and breathing rhythm. Getting to know it requires patience: its friendly appearance does not mean it always wants to be touched.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Move your mouse close to Luma Puff and pause to let it become comfortable. Watch its color brighten and its expression change, then click its body once to make it release bubbles. Avoid clicking repeatedly. Three quick clicks within one second will frighten it and make it swim away. If this happens, give it a quiet moment to return. Observe its reactions and let it set the pace of the interaction.
